Career Planning Checklist

Click items as you complete them to track your career readiness milestones.

  • ✓ Explore at least 5 different career profiles in the Career Explorer
  • ✓ Review university admission and entrance prerequisites for target degrees
  • ✓ Discuss aspirations openly with parents or a trusted mentor
  • ✓ Formulate a parallel secondary plan to avoid single-option anxiety
Tool 02

Interview Readiness Checklist

0 of 5 completed

Essential preparation steps before university admissions or first-job interviews.

  • ✓ Synthesize your academic narrative and "Why this field?" answer into 90 seconds
  • ✓ Structure 2 concrete case studies demonstrating problem-solving using the STAR method
  • ✓ Research the exact curriculum, recent faculty publications, or company initiatives
  • ✓ Prepare 3 insightful questions to ask the interviewer at the conclusion
  • ✓ Conduct at least one full mock conversational trial with a peer or counsellor
